Makerfield By-Election Could Decide A Prime Minister

  • The Makerfield by-election is unusual because its voters could effectively decide who becomes Labour leader and potentially Prime Minister.
  • The contest concentrates national attention on a handful of northwest mining towns, making momentum and perception disproportionately powerful in a by-election.
volunteer_activism ADVICE

Delay A Hasty Power Grab After A Surprise Win

  • Don't rush to claim the premiership immediately after a by-election win; take time to prepare policy and structure for government.
  • Sally Morgan recommended Burnham should avoid a hasty 'measuring the curtains' transition and focus on consolidating support first.
insights INSIGHT

Momentum Forces Leadership Contests To Happen Fast

  • Leadership change momentum often accelerates faster than planners expect because visible signals prompt rapid collective action.
  • Daniel Finkelstein highlights how journalists asking ministers about loyalty can quickly fracture a government and force an expedited contest.

We're only a week away from the Makerfield by-election, when voters could choose the next prime minister.


If Andy Burnham wins, what happens in the hours and days after he arrives in Westminster? Will he seize the moment and try to challenge Keir Starmer immediately, or wait until he has a plan for running the country?


And if he wins, what does it mean for Reform UK's electoral strategy?


We also look at Steve Hilton's journey from David Cameron's modernising adviser to the race to be the next governor of California.
